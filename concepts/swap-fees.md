# Fees

### Swaps

Native applies its fee to every firm quote it signs through the [Native RFQ API](../modules/native-relay.md). Quoted prices already include the fee for your API key, so the amount in a firm quote is what the user receives.

### Integration Partners

We collaborate with partners such as aggregators, wallets, and other taker/flow sources to establish customized fee rates based on individual requirements. The integrator fee is typically set at zero or minimal to keep quotes competitive.
