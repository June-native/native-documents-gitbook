# Tokenized Stocks & Equities (RWAs)

### Performance & Advantages

* **24/7 Trading:** Breaking the time constraints of Traditional Finance (TradFi), providing continuous on-chain liquidity and price discovery even during off-hours.
* **Advanced Depth & Spreads:** With orderbook infrastructure and PMMs, Native provides highly competitive depth and tight spreads on par with top-tier CEXs. Traders can execute large-size orders with greater certainty, without worrying about severe slippage.
* **Earning Sustainable Yields for RWA:** Earn yields while providing liquidity, especially for top-traded assets. This effectively lowering the overall cost of liquidity provisioning. The yield feature can also be an offering for asset holders and users, further increase utility, retention, and liquidity.&#x20;



### Case Study: bStocks

Native has supported bStocks since day one, delivering 24/7 liquidity, deep market depth, and tight spreads for retail users, professional traders, and liquidators.



**Leading DEX trading volume share for bStocks**

* One Month after bStocks' launch, Native has processed \~$34M in trading volume, executed 70K+ trades, and captured \~50% of total DEX trading volume on BNB Chain, demonstrating the competitiveness of the infrastructure.&#x20;

**Capital-efficient liquidity for bStocks**

* Native's CLOB and PMM infrastructure supports deep, capital-efficient bStocks markets without relying on a traditional AMM liquidity pair. This helps market makers quote competitively while users access liquidity through their preferred trading interface.
